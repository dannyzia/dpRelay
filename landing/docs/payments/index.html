How to pay for credits

Every payment ends at a bKash wallet. Pick a package, send the exact amount, then confirm your payment: paste the TrxID for a direct bKash payment, or follow the remittance steps below. We match it to the incoming bKash credit, and your credits appear.

Before you send

  1. Sign in and open Buy credits.
  2. Pick a package and choose your currency.
  3. Note the amount, the bKash number and the recipient name shown there. You'll enter all three in the steps below.

Send the exact amount shown so we can match your payment to the purchase. Check every digit of the bKash number: a transfer to a wrong but valid number can't be reversed.

Pay with bKash (BDT)

Best for: customers in Bangladesh.

  1. Open the bKash app and choose Send Money.
  2. Enter the bKash number shown on Buy credits.
  3. Enter the amount and confirm with your PIN.
  4. Copy the TrxID from the confirmation screen or SMS.
  5. Back on Buy credits, paste the TrxID and submit.

Pay from abroad (USD)

The services below deliver to a bKash wallet, and bKash lists each of them as a remittance partner. In each one, use the bKash number and recipient name from the Buy credits screen. Remittance senders don't receive a bKash TrxID, because bKash creates it, so there is nothing to paste.

Describe how remittance payments are matched, for example: send the exact BDT amount shown, by setting the "they receive" amount, then tell us the service and the time you sent it.

How to Send Using TapTap Send

Best for users in the US, UK, Canada, UAE, and Europe.

  1. Open the App: Download the TapTap Send app, log in, or set up your account.
  2. Select Destination: Pick Bangladesh from the country menu.
  3. Enter Amount: Type the amount you want to send. The app will display the current exchange rate and the total BDT the recipient will get.
  4. Add Recipient: Tap Add Recipient and choose bKash as the wallet provider.
  5. Input Details: Full Name: Enter the recipient's legal name. Phone Number: Input +8801613249520.
  6. Pay and Send: Link your local debit card, review the details, and tap Send.

How to Send Using Remitly

Best for competitive exchange rates and promotional offers.

  1. Log In: Open the Remitly app or website and sign in.
  2. Choose Country & Amount: Select Bangladesh as the destination and enter your sending amount.
  3. Select Delivery Speed: Choose Express for an instant transfer.
  4. Choose Delivery Method: Select Mobile Money and click on bKash.
  5. Recipient Info: Phone Number: Type in 01613249520. Recipient Name: Enter their legal first and last name.
  6. Complete Payment: Enter your card or bank payment details and hit Confirm & Send.

How to Send Using Wise

Best for getting the mid-market exchange rate with low, transparent fees.

  1. Set Up Transfer: Log into Wise and enter how much you want to transfer in your local currency.
  2. Select Recipient Type: Click on Someone else.
  3. Choose bKash Wallet: Enter the recipient's legal name and select the bKash payout option.
  4. Input Wallet Details: Mobile Number: Type the complete phone number: +8801613249520.
  5. Review & Pay: Review the transfer screen and pay using your linked bank account or debit card.

How to Send Using Western Union

Best for sending online or paying with cash at a brick-and-mortar location.

Option A: Via the Western Union App/Website

  1. Initiate Transfer: Go to Send Money and select Bangladesh.
  2. Select Payout: Choose Mobile Wallet as the reception method.
  3. Recipient Information: Add the recipient's legal name and enter +8801613249520 under the wallet number.
  4. Pay Online: Pay with your credit/debit card to send the money instantly.

Option B: In-Person at an Agent Location (Cash Payment)

  1. Visit an Agent: Go to a Western Union counter (e.g., in a grocery store, exchange house, or bank).
  2. Fill the Form: Write down the destination (Bangladesh), recipient's full name, and the bKash number: +8801613249520.
  3. Submit ID and Cash: Provide your local residency ID/passport and pay the agent the cash amount plus the transfer fee.
  4. Keep the Receipt: The agent will provide a receipt with an MTCN (Pin Number). The funds will route directly into the mobile wallet in minutes.

Western Union or Ria with a secret number (MTCN)

This is an alternative to sending straight to the wallet. You send the transfer as usual and give us the secret number (the MTCN), and we claim it into our bKash account. Because the transfer company checks it, this option suits larger payments, such as annual plans.

  1. On Buy credits, pick a package and choose Pay with a Western Union or Ria secret number. Note the exact BDT amount and the recipient name shown.
  2. In the Western Union or Ria app, send exactly that BDT amount to the recipient name shown, using the delivery option they specify.

    the delivery option to choose

  3. Copy the MTCN (secret number) from your confirmation.
  4. Back on Buy credits, enter the MTCN and the amount, then submit. Enter it only on that screen and don't share it with anyone else.
  5. We claim the transfer in bKash, and your credits are added once it succeeds. A person does this step. The claim itself can take up to 15 minutes to process.

How to Send Using WorldRemit

Available in over 50 countries for fast mobile wallet routing.

  1. Select Country: Open WorldRemit and choose Bangladesh.
  2. Select Service: Tap Mobile Money and choose bKash as the payment network.
  3. Enter Amount: Input the sum you wish to transfer and click Next.
  4. Enter Beneficiary Info: Type the recipient's full legal name and mobile number: 01613249520.
  5. Send Funds: Put in your international card details to authorize the transaction.

Ria, MoneyGram and ACE Money Transfer

bKash lists all three as partners for sending to a bKash account, but not from every country: its list doesn't show Ria for the US, UK or Canada, or ACE for the US or UAE.

Test one transfer with each and add its steps before publishing. In each, choose bKash or mobile wallet as the payout method, then enter the recipient name and the bKash number from Buy credits.

After you send

Open Buy credits, choose the same package, paste your TrxID (or, for a remittance, the details above) and submit. We match it to the incoming bKash payment. Matching is automatic when the details agree and checked by a person otherwise. Most payments are confirmed quickly. Follow progress under your transactions. If a TrxID is rejected as already used, check that you haven't pasted it against another pending purchase.
