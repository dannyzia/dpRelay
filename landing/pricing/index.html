Every message — OTP or bulk, either track — deducts one SMS credit from the same balance at the platform rate of ৳0.20 per credit. No per-country surcharges; the difference is the delivery infrastructure, not the price.

Credit packages

Packages are operator-managed plans, so sizes and bonuses change — the dashboard list is the live source of truth. Every package has the same shape:

  • Type — OTP-only, bulk-only, or a combined balance.
  • Credits — the SMS count added on approval, priced at ৳0.20 per credit in BDT terms.
  • Validity — a window the credits stay usable for; balances expire per package.
  • Currency — priced in BDT, USD, or EUR.

Free trial

Every new app starts with 20 free messages valid for 30 days — enough to wire up the OTP flow before spending anything.

Ways to pay

  • BDT — bKash Send Money, then submit the TrxID on the Buy credits screen.
  • USD / EUR — any of five remittance services route to the bKash wallet: TapTap Send, Remitly, Wise, Western Union, WorldRemit.
  • TrxIDs are confirmed by a human operator — credits appear once the match is approved.
