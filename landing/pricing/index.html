Pricing for the way you send

20 free messages for 30 days. No card required.

Two ways to send with dP Relay: BYOD&S — bring your own device and SIM — or Turn Key, managed delivery for supported Bangladesh destinations. Both are paid from your dP Relay Wallet.

BYOD&S

Bring your own device and SIM

Send from an Android phone and SIM you control. Pay as you go at $0.0015 per message from your Wallet, or choose an Unlimited plan for predictable pricing. Your mobile operator's SMS charges are separate and always yours.

What “unlimited” means: An Unlimited plan does not charge a dP Relay per-message gateway fee for eligible normal business use under the plan's fair-use rules. It does not remove mobile-carrier charges, carrier rate limits, device constraints or applicable messaging rules.

Unlimited plans apply to the BYOD&S gateway service; the Turn Key managed route is billed separately at its published per-message rate.

How payment works

Your Wallet balance funds pay-as-you-go sending and Unlimited plan purchases. Buy credits in the dashboard is the top-up flow.

  1. Sign in and open Buy credits.
  2. Choose the available package, route and currency.
  3. Use the exact amount and recipient details shown in the purchase flow.
  4. Send the payment using one of the methods offered to your account.
  5. Submit the requested transaction reference or remittance details and follow the status in your transaction list.

Payments currently advertised include bKash and selected remittance services. Availability depends on the sending country and the live options shown to your account. A payment may require manual review before credits are added.

About competitor comparisons

We do not publish a live competitor price table on this page by default. Plans differ in how they count messages, handle SMS segments, charge for carrier delivery, limit devices and define “unlimited.” A table of named vendors can become misleading when even one price or feature changes. If a named comparison is added later, use official current sources, a visible verification date and a clear explanation of differences in service model.
