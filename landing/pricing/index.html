Credit packages

Credits are sold in packages, and the exact sizes and any bonus credits are managed by the operator, so the live list in your dashboard is always current. Every package has four properties:

  • Type: OTP-only, bulk-only, or a combined balance.
  • Credits: the number of messages added when your payment is approved.
  • Validity: how long those credits stay usable. Unused credits expire when the package window ends.
  • Currency: priced in USD (phone track) or BDT (Bangladesh numbers).

How to pay

  • In BDT: send money with bKash, then paste the TrxID on the Buy credits screen. The destination number and exact amount are shown there after you pick a package.
  • In USD: send through TapTap Send, Remitly, Wise, Western Union or WorldRemit. All five deliver to bKash, so the steps after sending are the same.
  • Approval: a person on our team confirms each payment. You can follow the status in your transaction list.

typical approval time

About fees: each remittance service charges its own fee, paid to that service and not to dP Relay, so compare a few before you send. Choose the option where the fee is added on top of the amount, because the amount we receive has to match the package price.
