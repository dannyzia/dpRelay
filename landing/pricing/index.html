Monthly own-phone gateway plans

These plans cover dP Relay's gateway service for the number of enrolled phones shown. Your mobile operator's SMS charges, plan limits and fair-use terms remain separate.

PlanIncluded phonesMonthly gateway price

What “unlimited” means: The plan does not charge a dP Relay per-message gateway fee for eligible normal business use under the plan's fair-use rules. It does not remove mobile-carrier charges, carrier rate limits, device constraints or applicable messaging rules.

Managed Bangladesh delivery is billed separately at its published per-message rate; it is not included in these own-phone plans.

Message credits

If you use pay-as-you-go or a route that requires credits, your account shows the packages currently available to you. Review the following before purchasing:

  • Route or credit type: Which sending route and message types the package supports.
  • Message allowance: how many billable messages the package adds.
  • Validity: When the credits expire, if an expiry applies.
  • Currency and total: The exact currency and amount to pay.
  • Approval: Whether a manual payment review is required before credits are added.

Use the live package list in the dashboard for current quantities, bonuses and validity.

How payment works

  1. Sign in and open Buy credits.
  2. Choose the available package, route and currency.
  3. Use the exact amount and recipient details shown in the purchase flow.
  4. Send the payment using one of the methods offered to your account.
  5. Submit the requested transaction reference or remittance details and follow the status in your transaction list.

Payments currently advertised include bKash and selected remittance services. Availability depends on the sending country and the live options shown to your account. A payment may require manual review before credits are added.

About competitor comparisons

We do not publish a live competitor price table on this page by default. Plans differ in how they count messages, handle SMS segments, charge for carrier delivery, limit devices and define “unlimited.” A table of named vendors can become misleading when even one price or feature changes. If a named comparison is added later, use official current sources, a visible verification date and a clear explanation of differences in service model.
