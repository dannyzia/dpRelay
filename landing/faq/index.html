Frequently asked questions

Quick answers on getting started, billing, delivery and using your own phone. Can't find yours? Contact us.

Getting started

Which delivery track should I choose?

Choose your own phone for worldwide reach and your own sender number. Choose our dedicated Bangladesh numbers to reach +880 numbers on Grameenphone, Robi, Banglalink and Teletalk without running any hardware. Both use the same API.

How do I get API keys?

Sign in with your email, then register an app on the Apps screen. The appId and appSecret are shown once, so store them safely. If an operator issued you an app, use Link existing app and enter those credentials.

How do I reset my password?

On the sign-in screen choose Forgot password? and enter your email; a reset link follows. If email isn't enabled yet on this deployment, contact us and we can reset it for you.

Billing and payments

What does a message cost?

On your own phone, $0.0015 per message (USD only), for OTP or bulk, in any country, or unlimited sending from $16 a month. On our dedicated Bangladesh numbers, ৳0.20 per message with sending included. New apps start with 20 free messages valid for 30 days. After that you buy packages; see pricing.

How do I pay?

Pick a package on the Buy credits screen, send the money to the bKash number shown there, then paste the TrxID. From abroad you can use TapTap Send, Remitly, Wise, Western Union or WorldRemit. The payment guides walk through each.

How long until my credits appear?

A person on our team confirms every payment. Credits are added as soon as it is approved.

typical approval time

Do credits expire?

Yes. Each package has a validity window, and unused credits expire when it ends. The validity is shown on the package before you buy.

Why was my TrxID rejected?

The most common cause is a TrxID already attached to another pending purchase. Check you haven't pasted it twice, and that you used the same package you paid for. If it still fails, contact us with the TrxID.

Delivery

Why hasn't my SMS arrived?

Check that the number is strict E.164 (for example +8801XXXXXXXXX) and that the recipient's phone is on and has signal. Failed sends appear in the History screen. If many sends fail together, the sending device is usually offline; on your own phone, check it first, otherwise contact us.

Why am I getting a 429 error?

You hit a limit that protects deliverability: rate_limited means too many sends to one number, and resend_cooldown means you asked for another code too soon. Wait and retry, and disable your resend button during the cooldown. See rate limits.

A user typed the wrong code too many times. What now?

The number is locked and the API returns otp_locked with retryAfterSec. After that time passes, send a new code.

Using your own phone

Can I use my own phone as the gateway?

Yes. Enroll any Android phone and messages go out over your SIM, on any carrier, in any country it can reach. Each message is billed at $0.0015. See Connect your phone.

Does the phone need to stay on?

Yes. Keep it powered, connected and allowed to run the gateway app in the background. If it goes offline, sends fail until it is back.

What if I lose my enrollment secret?

Enroll the device again to get a new secret and paste it into the gateway app right away. It pairs your phone to your account and is not your password.
