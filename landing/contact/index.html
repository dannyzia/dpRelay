Talk to a person

Contact the dP Relay team about account access, a payment that has not appeared, a delivery problem or an API integration question.

Email support

Email admin@dprelay.digital-papyrus.com with your transaction reference (TrxID) for account access, payment verification, or integration questions. We aim to reply within one business day.

WhatsApp support

Chat directly with our support team on WhatsApp for account access, payment verification, or integration questions.

Help us investigate

Depending on the issue, include:

  • Your app ID, but never your app secret.
  • The transaction reference for a payment question.
  • The recipient number in international format and the approximate time of a failed send.
  • Which delivery route you were using.
  • The relevant error code or request ID, if available.

Do not send OTP values, passwords, app secrets or unnecessary recipient data in a support request.
