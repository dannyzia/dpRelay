What you can build with dP Relay

Verify users with OTPs

Add phone verification to registration, sign-in, checkout or sensitive account actions. Generate a code, send it through an eligible route, and verify the code entered by the user.

Send useful business messages

Send reminders, receipts, delivery updates and other transactional messages. For promotions and other marketing messages, contact recipients only where you have the required consent and follow applicable opt-out rules.

Send bulk campaigns from the dashboard

Prepare and manage bulk campaigns in the dashboard using the workflow supported by your account. The current documented bulk workflow is dashboard-based.

Send operational alerts

Use SMS for selected alerts from internal tools, deployments, devices or monitoring systems. For critical alerts, design retries and backup channels rather than relying on a single phone or route.

Why teams choose dP Relay

Keep control of your sending setup

With the own-phone route, messages go through a phone and SIM you control. Sender display depends on the destination network and carrier, but you retain control of the sending device and mobile plan.

Start small

Use the free trial to test your integration before committing to a larger plan. Choose pay-as-you-go gateway pricing while volume is low or variable; compare the monthly gateway plans when your usage becomes steady.

Choose the route that fits

Use your own phone for destinations your SIM can reach, or choose managed delivery where supported. The two routes have different requirements and pricing, so compare them before sending.

Integrate the OTP workflow

Use the documented API endpoints to send a code, verify it and check its status. Keep your app secret on your server and follow the documented rate limits and resend cooldowns.

How it works

  1. Create your app. Sign up, create an app and keep your API credentials securely on your server.
  2. Choose a delivery route. Enrol an Android phone for the own-phone gateway, or use managed delivery if it is enabled for your account and destination.
  3. Send and verify. Call the documented OTP endpoints from your backend. For bulk campaigns, use the dashboard workflow currently supported by dP Relay.

Start with two API calls

Use POST /v5/otp/send to request an OTP, then POST /v5/otp/verify to check the code entered by the user. See the Quickstart for authentication, request examples, responses and error handling.

A few things to know

  • An API request being accepted does not necessarily mean the SMS was delivered.
  • The own-phone route depends on your Android device, SIM, signal, mobile plan and carrier limits.
  • Managed delivery is available only for supported destinations and routes.
  • Carrier filtering and destination restrictions can apply to any sender. dP Relay does not bypass carrier rules or guarantee delivery.
