How it works

  1. Create your account

    Sign up with your email, verify it, and land in the dashboard — new apps receive free trial credits.

  2. Register an app

    The dashboard issues your appId and appSecret once. They are the only credentials every API call needs.

  3. Send messages

    Call POST /v5/otp/send for verification codes or launch a bulk campaign from the dashboard. Balance updates as credits are used.

Built for developers

  • Strict E.164 numbers, clear error codes, and per-number rate limits that protect deliverability.
  • Signed webhooks (X-DP-Signature) so your backend can trust delivery events.
