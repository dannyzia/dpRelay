Your Android phone is the SMS gateway — or use ours

dP Relay delivers OTP codes and bulk campaigns over one REST API: through a device you own, anywhere in the world, or through our dedicated Bangladesh SIM pool.

Worldwide reach on a device you own

Enroll any Android phone as your app's gateway and send through its SIM — any carrier, any country your phone can reach. Your carrier plan carries delivery; the platform meters each message from your credit balance.

  1. Install the dP Relay gateway app on the phone that holds your SIM.
  2. Paste the device enrollment secret from the dashboard — the app registers itself to your account.
  3. Call the API; messages go out over your own number.

How it works

  1. Create your app

    Sign up, verify your email, register an app — the appId and appSecret are issued once. New apps start with free trial credits.

  2. Connect a gateway

    Enroll your own Android phone from the dashboard, or rely on the operator's Bangladesh SIM pool — both deliver through the same API.

  3. Send messages

    Call POST /v5/otp/send for codes or launch bulk campaigns from the dashboard. Balance updates as credits are used.

Built for developers

  • Plain REST with JSON bodies and predictable { ok, error, code } failures.
  • Strict E.164 numbers, clear error codes, per-number rate limits that protect deliverability.
  • Signed webhooks (X-DP-Signature) so your backend can trust delivery events.
  • Transparent per-message credits with package top-ups — see pricing and the API docs.
