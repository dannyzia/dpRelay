What makes dP Relay different?

Traditional bulk SMS aggregators push your messages through shared high-volume routes and sender IDs that carriers and recipients treat with suspicion. dP Relay takes a different approach: your messages leave from a normal mobile number.

A real number, not a short code

On the own-phone route, messages are sent from the SIM in your enrolled Android phone. Recipients see a familiar mobile number rather than an anonymous bulk sender, so your OTPs and updates look like the messages people expect — and your brand is not fighting the spam folder before it is even read.

You own the sending identity

The number stays yours. Keep the customer relationships, replies and call-back channel that come with a real phone number, and move providers without losing the identity your customers already saved.

Two routes, one API

Send through your own phone where your SIM can reach, or switch on managed Bangladesh delivery for a turnkey route. Same API, same dashboard — pick the route per destination instead of juggling providers.

What can you use it for?

OTP verification

Add phone verification to registration, sign-in, checkout or sensitive account actions. Generate a code, send it through an eligible route, and verify the code entered by the user.

Customer updates

Send reminders, receipts, delivery updates and other transactional messages people actually read, from a number they can reply to.

Promotional messages

Run offers and campaigns with consent at the centre: contact recipients only where you have the required permission and follow applicable opt-out rules.

Operational alerts

Use SMS for selected alerts from internal tools, deployments, devices or monitoring systems. For critical alerts, design retries and backup channels rather than relying on a single phone or route.

How it works

  1. Create your app. Sign up, create an app and keep your API credentials securely on your server.
  2. Choose a delivery route. Enrol an Android phone for the own-phone gateway, or use managed delivery if it is enabled for your account and destination.
  3. Send and verify. Call the documented OTP endpoints from your backend. For bulk campaigns, use the dashboard workflow currently supported by dP Relay.

Start with two API calls

Use POST /v5/otp/send to request an OTP, then POST /v5/otp/verify to check the code entered by the user. See the Quickstart for authentication, request examples, responses and error handling.
