Privacy policy

Draft for review: a lawyer should review these privacy terms before they go live. Where a section still needs a lawyer, it is marked as draft for review, lawyer to finalise.

Sections

  1. What we collect. Your account email, app details, the recipient numbers you send to, message delivery records, enrolled device details, and payment TrxIDs. Message bodies are stored as delivery records for the life of your account.

    Whether message text is stored, and for how long — draft for review, lawyer to finalise.

  2. How we use it. To deliver messages, bill credits, prevent abuse and support you.
  3. OTP codes. OTP codes are stored as a per-session salted hash, not as plaintext — the raw code is unrecoverable after creation. Codes expire after 5 minutes; the session record (status and timestamps) remains as a delivery record.

    Say exactly how long codes are kept and whether they are stored hashed — draft for review, lawyer to finalise.

  4. Where it is stored. The service runs on Render (Singapore region), with a SQLite database and continuous Litestream backups to Cloudflare R2.

    Hosting provider and region, backup arrangements — draft for review, lawyer to finalise.

  5. Who we share it with. Carriers and gateway devices needed to deliver a message, and infrastructure providers that run the service. We don't sell personal data.

    Add any others — draft for review, lawyer to finalise.

  6. Retention. Delivery records are retained until the operator purges them after an account is closed — there is no automatic purge today.

    Retention period for delivery records and for closed accounts — draft for review, lawyer to finalise.

  7. Your choices. To request access, correction or deletion of your data, contact us through the route on contact.

    How to request access, correction or deletion — draft for review, lawyer to finalise.

  8. Contact.

    Privacy contact email — draft for review, lawyer to finalise.
