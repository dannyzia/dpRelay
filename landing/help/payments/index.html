Add credits to your dP Relay account

Payment guides for every supported method. Every purchase shows you the exact recipient, amount and currency before you pay. Pay with bKash inside Bangladesh, or with an international remittance service from abroad. After paying, you submit the transaction reference in your dashboard and credits are added once the payment is confirmed.

Before you pay

  1. Sign in and open Buy credits.
  2. Choose the package and route you need.
  3. Confirm the currency, exact amount, recipient name and destination shown on the screen.
  4. Use only the payment method presented for that purchase.

Send the exact amount shown. Check the recipient details carefully before confirming the transfer; a payment sent to the wrong recipient may not be recoverable.

Pay with bKash (Bangladesh)

Send Money to the destination number displayed on the Buy credits screen — it is shown again on every bKash purchase.

  1. Open the bKash app and choose Send Money.
  2. Enter the destination number displayed in Buy credits.
  3. Enter the exact amount and verify the recipient details before confirming.
  4. Save the transaction ID (TrxID) shown after the payment.
  5. Return to Buy credits and submit the TrxID in the matching purchase.
  6. Follow the transaction status until the payment is approved or the dashboard requests more information.

Pay from abroad with an international remittance service

All five services below deliver to your bKash wallet at the destination number displayed in Buy credits. Pick the one that operates in your country — the recipient details are the same for all of them: recipient name as on your account, the destination mobile number shown in Buy credits, payout via bKash wallet, destination Bangladesh. If the payment cannot be matched automatically, support may need to review it.

TapTap Send

Best for users in the US, UK, Canada, UAE and Europe.

  1. Open the App: Download the TapTap Send app, log in, or set up your account.
  2. Select Destination: Pick Bangladesh from the country menu.
  3. Enter Amount: Type the amount you want to send. The app will display the current exchange rate and the total BDT the recipient will get.
  4. Add Recipient: Tap Add Recipient and choose bKash as the wallet provider.
  5. Input Details: Full Name: Enter the recipient's legal name. Phone Number: Input the destination number displayed in Buy credits.
  6. Pay and Send: Link your local debit card, review the details, and tap Send.

Remitly

Best for competitive exchange rates and promotional offers.

  1. Log In: Open the Remitly app or website and sign in.
  2. Choose Country & Amount: Select Bangladesh as the destination and enter your sending amount.
  3. Select Delivery Speed: Choose Express for an instant transfer.
  4. Choose Delivery Method: Select Mobile Money and click on bKash.
  5. Recipient Info: Phone Number: Type in the destination number displayed in Buy credits. Recipient Name: Enter their legal first and last name.
  6. Complete Payment: Enter your card or bank payment details and hit Confirm & Send.

Wise

Best for getting the mid-market exchange rate with low, transparent fees.

  1. Set Up Transfer: Log into Wise and enter how much you want to transfer in your local currency.
  2. Select Recipient Type: Click on Someone else.
  3. Choose bKash Wallet: Enter the recipient's legal name and select the bKash payout option.
  4. Input Wallet Details: Mobile Number: Type the complete destination number displayed in Buy credits.
  5. Review & Pay: Review the transfer screen and pay using your linked bank account or debit card.

Western Union

Best for sending online or paying with cash at a brick-and-mortar location.

Option A: Via the Western Union App/Website

  1. Initiate Transfer: Go to Send Money and select Bangladesh.
  2. Select Payout: Choose Mobile Wallet as the reception method.
  3. Recipient Information: Add the recipient's legal name and enter the destination number displayed in Buy credits under the wallet number.
  4. Pay Online: Pay with your credit/debit card to send the money instantly.

Option B: In-Person at an Agent Location (Cash Payment)

  1. Visit an Agent: Go to a Western Union counter (e.g., in a grocery store, exchange house, or bank).
  2. Fill the Form: Write down the destination (Bangladesh), recipient's full name, and the destination number displayed in Buy credits.
  3. Submit ID and Cash: Provide your local residency ID/passport and pay the agent the cash amount plus the transfer fee.
  4. Keep the Receipt: The agent will provide a receipt with an MTCN (Pin Number). The funds will route directly into the mobile wallet in minutes.

WorldRemit

Available in over 50 countries for fast mobile wallet routing.

  1. Select Country: Open WorldRemit and choose Bangladesh.
  2. Select Service: Tap Mobile Money and choose bKash as the payment network.
  3. Enter Amount: Input the sum you wish to transfer and click Next.
  4. Enter Beneficiary Info: Type the recipient's full legal name and the destination mobile number displayed in Buy credits.
  5. Send Funds: Put in your international card details to authorize the transaction.

Do not assume that a remittance transaction has a bKash TrxID. Follow the method-specific instructions in the purchase flow. Do not send confidential transfer references outside the official payment form or verified support process.

After paying

Open Buy credits, pick your package, and submit the transaction reference: the TrxID from a bKash confirmation SMS, or the remittance reference your service issued. Credits are added once the payment is confirmed — most references confirm automatically.

Payment status

A payment may require manual review before credits are added. Your transaction list shows the current status. If it remains pending beyond the published processing window, contact support with the transaction reference and the package you selected. Do not include your app secret.

Common problems

My TrxID was rejected. Check that you submitted the reference to the matching purchase and did not already attach it to another pending transaction. If it still fails, contact support with the reference.

The amount received is different. Exchange rates and transfer fees can affect the amount delivered. Review the purchase instructions and transfer receipt, then contact support rather than submitting random amounts against multiple packages.

I cannot see my payment method. Availability depends on country, currency and the current purchase flow. Use a method actually offered in your account or contact support.
